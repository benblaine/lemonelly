Before We Build

Let's nail your story.

Cookie-cutter templates don't win customers. Real understanding does. The deeper we dive into what makes your business tick, who you serve, and what sets you apart, the sharper your copy gets — and the harder your new site works to drive high-quality leads.

Step 1: Start your Free Draft

No clunky accounts or passwords to juggle just yet. We like to get straight to the point. Your first move is simple: tell us what you want.

  • Share your vision and goals
  • Give us the raw details of your business
  • Watch us spin it into a custom Free Draft

No upfront commitments. Just your ideas, fast-tracked.

Prep for impact

We move fast, but a little prep goes a long way. To hit the ground running, try to gather:

  • Your best project photos ready to upload
  • Quick, rough notes for each page

Not a writer? Don't stress. Bullet points are all we need — or send us rambling voice notes about your business and what you want, and we'll turn them into content.

Your site's blueprint

Most heavy-hitting small-business sites rock 6 to 8 core pages. The standard arsenal includes:

  • A high-converting Home page
  • An authentic About Us page
  • A stunning Project Gallery to show off your work
  • SEO-optimized Service pages (so you actually rank on Google)
  • A Contact page engineered to generate inquiries

Need more? Accreditations, Pricing, Case Studies, or Meet the Team? You got it. Bespoke means built exclusively for you.

The anti-template advantage

Anyone can prompt an AI to spit out a generic layout. We dig deeper. We ask the tough, agency-level questions that uncover exactly why clients choose you over the competition.

  • We brief you the same way top agencies brief national brands
  • We transform your raw answers into proper, lead-generating copy
  • Your site is 100% custom-built — never recycled, never templated

That's why a lemonelly site crushes a drag-and-drop Wix build. The real power is in the strategy we build before we write a single line of code.

Common questions

Nothing's free. What's the catch?

There isn't one. We carry the cost of the draft because most people who see their finished site want to keep it. If you don't, you walk away and owe nothing.

What does a website cost with lemonelly?

Nothing upfront. The design, the draft, and every revision are free. You only start paying R2,200/month once you're happy and your new site goes live.

How fast will my site be live?

Seven days from kickoff. Days 1–2 we design your free draft, days 3–5 we refine it with your feedback, days 6–7 we go live.

What does the R2,200/month actually cover?

Everything: fast hosting, SSL security, software and security updates, one round of content edits every month — new photos, price changes, fresh copy — plus SEO and direct WhatsApp support. No upfront cost. No hidden fees. Cancel anytime.

What do you need from me to get started?

A phone number. We call you, agree a time, and take it from there. Point us at your current website if you have one.

Can I change things after the site is live?

Yes. One round of updates is included every single month, so your site keeps up as your business grows — just send us what's changed.
