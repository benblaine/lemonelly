## Services (nav + service pages, 2026-07-28)
- Pitched roofing — clay tiles, concrete tiles, natural slate; tiling, re-roofs,
  and repairs (services/pitched-roofing)
- Flat roofing — built-up bituminous felt, GRP fibreglass (RAL colour matched),
  insulated/warm roofs, green roofs (services/flat-roofing)
- Roofline — fascias, soffits, guttering and rainwater goods; "elements that
  support and transport the flow of water off and away from your roof"
  (services/roofline)
- Balcony installations — "safe and secure balconies and roof terraces with
  anti-slip finishes" (services/balcony-installations)
- Rooflights — "high quality skylights, roof windows and sun tunnels"
  (services/rooflights)
- Roof repair — slipped/broken tiles & slates, leaking valleys/flashings, timber
  and chimney/ridge repairs (roof-repair, pitched-roofing)
